Every marathon has a moment around 30 km where the pace you set at the start either holds or it doesn't. Tempo Gear has been running at that pace for five years. The question for anyone watching the business is whether the second half will be faster than the first.

## The business in plain English

Tempo Gear makes running shoes and a small line of apparel. It sells through three channels:

- **Wholesale**: specialist running shops and big sports retailers. High volume, lower margin.
- **Direct-to-consumer (DTC)**: its own website and a handful of flagship stores. Lower volume, but the company keeps the retailer's cut.
- **Distributors** in markets where it has no local team.

The key idea is **gross margin**, which is what's left of each dollar of sales after paying to make the product. Shift sales from wholesale to DTC and gross margin goes up, because Tempo keeps the retailer's slice. That shift is most of the story here.

## The numbers that matter

| Metric | FY24 | FY25 | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Revenue | $602m | $655m | +8.8% |
| Gross margin | 45.1% | 47.0% | +1.9 pts |
| Operating margin | 11.2% | 12.4% | +1.2 pts |
| DTC share of sales | 34% | 39% | +5 pts |
| Free cash flow | $41m | $58m | +41% |

Three things stand out:

1. **DTC share is driving margin.** Every five points of mix shift has added about two points of gross margin.
2. **Inventory is clean.** Stock grew more slowly than sales, so there's little risk of discounting.
3. **Cash conversion improved.** Free cash flow (cash from operations minus capital spending) grew far faster than revenue.

## Bull vs bear

**The bull case.** DTC keeps climbing toward 50% of sales, gross margin closes half the gap to Peer A, and operating margin reaches the mid-teens. At a steady valuation multiple, earnings growth does the work.

**The bear case.** Running is a fashion business wearing a performance costume. One flat product cycle and wholesale partners cut orders, inventory builds, and the margin gains of the last two years unwind in a single season of discounting.

Both cases rest on the same variable: whether the next shoe launches land.
